IMPOSTOR

Find the impostor among the crew

How to play
  1. Everyone gets the same secret word — except the impostor, who gets a related word and knows they're the impostor.
  2. Take turns saying one clue about your word. Don't make it too obvious!
  3. When the host opens voting, vote for who you think the impostor is.
  4. Impostor caught: the crew scores. Impostor escapes: the impostor scores.
